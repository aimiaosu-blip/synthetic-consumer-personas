MAE/RMSE and bias use the mean prediction per pair. Exact/within-one agreement compare individual draws with the reference. Stability is sample SD within a pair and pairwise exact agreement across runs; unavailable for one run. Spearman uses concept means, with average ranks for ties; undefined for constant ranks. CI is a 500-resample respondent-cluster percentile bootstrap.

Interpretation boundary

Synthetic benchmarks test the pipeline only. Shared assumptions between generators can inflate agreement. A real-public label reflects the supplied provenance declaration, not independent verification. Scores are ordinal; numeric distances assume equal spacing. Three concepts give very limited ranking evidence. Small cells are descriptive, and no score demonstrates that AI can replace consumers.

Feedback text is exported for qualitative review. Automated semantic validity and demographic fairness have not been established. No company data or real research findings are included in the shipped demonstration.
